# M4 semantic-facet extraction — implementation and private full-data audit (2026-11-10)

## Scope and design

- Source contract: [Master Duel basic design](../docs/BASIC_DESIGN.md), M4 milestone of [roadmap](../docs/INITIAL_GOAL_REMAINING_ROADMAP_20261110.md). First goal: evidence-bearing semantic candidates, not a ruling/gameplay engine, all-card theme resolution or automatic deck recommendations.
- Extractor `effect_semantics_m4/extraction.py`: deterministic, clause-aware lexical candidates for DRAW, SEARCH_FROM_DECK, RECOVER_FROM_GRAVE, SPECIAL_SUMMON, NEGATE, DESTROY, BANISH, SEND_TO_GRAVE, RETURN_TO_HAND, TRIBUTE, DISCARD; once-per-turn, costs, activation conditions, summon locks, quoted names and broad attribute/race/level targets.
- **Crucial separation**: candidate action `ACTION`, target/trigger `CONDITION`, quoted other effects `REFERENCED`, lock/limit `RESTRICTION`, and evidence-only `METADATA`. Every facet has the exact source-text `[start,end)` offsets, text SHA256, source, version, extractor version, confidence and `UNVERIFIED_INTERPRETATION`.
- M1 literal search and M3 banlist/CP remain intact; M4 does not claim to resolve full rulings, exact activation legality or guarantee synergy. Missing extracted facet means *NO_MATCH*, not proof that a card cannot perform the action. `PATTERN_HIGH` is a pattern match, **not official verified gameplay**.
- A separate private sidecar SQLite `effect_semantics_m4/index.py` is built from a pin-verified read-only source SQLite. Production `MD_CURRENT_HEAD.json`, ZIP, inventory, regulation snapshot, and card texts are unchanged. Sidecar reads are SHA-versioned and reject stale source versions. SQL queries accept only predefined categories/roles and parameterized filters.

## Limits and next review

- Pattern-based semantics can miss paraphrases, overtag long clauses, or mistake timing/agency in composite conditions. No claim of semantic ground truth or rulings-level coverage.
- M4 stage-2 quality work: manually label a private, balanced sample, add precision/recall by facet and source span, triage false positives, improve multi-clause and referent target binding; prioritize cards used in M5/M6 recommendations. All samples are private, no actual effect excerpts in public tests.
- Full archetype/synergy inference and engine constraints belong to M5; recipe synthesis to M6. The user's approved `UNKNOWN` craftability provisional GO and M3 regulation candidate state remain unchanged.
