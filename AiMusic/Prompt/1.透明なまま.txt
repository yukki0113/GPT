●Style

Female lead vocal, clear youthful tone, emotional and uplifting.
Catchy pre-chorus build-up into a big sing-along chorus.

Tempo: mid-to-up (around 160 BPM feel), driving rhythm.
Start the chorus with the strongest hook line.
