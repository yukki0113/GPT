## Acceptance and limitations
- P0-1 **per-year static coverage and exception accounting: PASS** for all ten source-year windows. 2025 is 99.9665% (11,926 / 11,930 unique horses), not 100%.
- P0-1 **historic as-of reconstruction: PARTIAL**, because annual UKC holds selected snapshots rather than full historical observation history. An unverified as-of profile does not mean the horse lacks pedigree.
- KYI counts are **declared entries**, not verified actual starters. SED result and cancellation join not yet part of this audit.
- Parent canonical-ID resolution / depth 0–5 and regional root taxonomy are **not measured** here; unresolved does not equal FALSE.
- P0-2 may reuse verified static facts for descriptive research but must not use later-observed profiles as race-date predictors. Full pre-race-safe historical backtests require temporal source validation.
- **No change** to BRDB CURRENT, PWA, Newspaper, RaceNote, frozen Blood Review v0.1 or production serving.
