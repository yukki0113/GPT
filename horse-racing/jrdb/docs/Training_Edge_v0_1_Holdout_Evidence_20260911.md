## 7. Pre-specified decision rule and classification

The freeze document defined:

- `CONFIRMED`: clear positive ranking/spread retained and processed-only baseline improved in both years, or strongly pooled without material year reversal;
- `PARTIAL`: signal remains positive but incremental gain is inconsistent by year;
- `FAILED`: ranking/spread materially collapses or reverses.

Observed holdout evidence satisfies the `CONFIRMED` condition:

1. Spearman is positive and higher than processed-only in both 2024 and 2025.
2. Top-minus-bottom mean spread is positive and larger than processed-only in both years.
3. Top-minus-bottom median spread is positive and larger than processed-only in both years.
4. Positive-delta-rate spread is positive and larger than processed-only in both years.
5. RMSE also improves slightly in both years.
6. There is no material year reversal between 2024 and 2025.
